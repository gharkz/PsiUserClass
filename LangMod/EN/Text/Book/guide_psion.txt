The Psion's Primer,an unnamed Psion

Few have heard of us, and fewer still can teach what we do. Our power is not drawn from magic. It comes from the mind itself.

<b>Psicraft</b>

Psicraft is our discipline. It grows as you use your powers, and as it grows, new powers awaken in you. No trainer can teach it.

Spellbooks of powers you already command will not grant you stock. Their insight flows into your Psicraft instead.

{p}

<b>Awakening</b>

{PSION_AWAKENING}

Your powers are innate. They cost mana, never stock.

{p}

<b>Mind and force</b>

Psi powers strike the mind. They may confuse a foe or lull it to sleep, which leaves it open to your staff or blade.

Kinetic powers strike with raw force. Armor (PV) and physical damage reduction blunt them, as any blow; elemental damage reduction does not.

{p}

<b>Shield and step</b>

Psi-Shield: the same ward as Holy Shield. They don't stack; the stronger holds.

Psi-Teleport: pick any open spot on this map. Blocked or occupied, you land at random. Cancel to keep your mana.

{p}

<b>Hurl</b>

Psi-Hurl flings anything you can see, yourself included. Right-click the target, then right-click where it should fly: it travels in a line from its own tile, like a bolt cast from there. Left or middle click cancels at no cost.

{PSION_HURL_FLIGHT} It shares Telekinesis's level and grants Telekinesis experience on use. {PSION_HURL_COST}

{p}

<b>Hurl: caveats</b>

Collision damage is the dice roll x (50% + 25% per tile flown). The dice undersell it:

* 4 tiles: 150%
* 8 tiles: 250%
* 20 tiles: 550%

Distant, heavy and higher-level targets resist more often.

{PSION_HURL_DANGER}

{p}

<b>Telekinetic Control</b>

* Rank 1 (Psicraft {PSION_TKC_LV1}): your self-hurl collisions hurt half as much; when others hurl you, 10% less.

{p}

<b>Strike</b>

Psi-Strike carries your ordinary melee attack to a target up to 8 tiles away in line of sight (no farther than you can see; dark places shorten it), like a blade thrown and called back. Your weapons or martial arts work exactly as in a normal attack, with the same damage and skill experience. Psicraft and the ability's level add nothing. {PSION_STRIKE_COST}

Weapon traits work as if you stood on the square just before the target: a weapon that hits adjacent enemies sweeps the foes around the target, and consecutive hits land as usual.

{p}

<b>The dead remember</b>

Never reach into the mind of the dead. Whatever harm your mind would deal them lashes back into you.

Telekinesis and kinetic powers are safe against them. In a Psion's hands, Telekinesis collision dice have {PSION_TKMULT} the sides a spellbook user's do, at {PSION_TKMP}. Wands, rods and scrolls carry their own power and are safe as well.

{p}

<b>Necropathy</b>

With practice, a Psion learns to touch dead minds without harm.

* Necropathy (Psicraft {PSION_NECRO_LV1}): the dead no longer lash back.
* Intermediate Necropathy (Psicraft {PSION_NECRO_LV2}): your mind reaches them at half strength.
* Master Necropathy (Psicraft {PSION_NECRO_LV3}): your mind reaches them fully.

Rare equipment is said to carry this gift as well.

{p}

<b>Psionic Edge</b>

Your mind steadies your hands. The edge adds to your harvesting tools' effective hardness, and to the level of any craft or fine skill you have learned when you use it. It grows with Psicraft:

* Psicraft 1: +1
* Psicraft 5: +2
* Psicraft 10: +3
* Psicraft 20: +4
* Psicraft 30: +5
* Psicraft 52: +6
* Psicraft 100: +7
* Psicraft 200: +8

At Psicraft 5 (+2), a basalt pickaxe (hardness 52) hits as if it were 54, and carpentry 10 works as carpentry 12 when you craft a chair.

Note: it does not count toward the skill needed to learn recipes by dismantling.

{p}

<b>Settings</b>

The strategy view on your character sheet has a Psion settings button.

* Auto-combat Telekinesis: don't use (default), pull distant foes only, hurl adjacent foes only, or both. Auto-combat skips a cast whose path holds any bystander kind you have checked among non-hostiles (allies, neutral animals and monsters, neutral NPCs, friendly NPCs, evolved monsters, Big Daddy). Hostiles never block it. It can also be limited to casts that deal collision damage (hurls), optionally allowing a pull that sets up a hurl.

{p}

* Telekinesis collision dice sides: x1.5 by default, any positive number up to 10.
* Telekinesis mana cost: x0.5 by default, 0 (free) up to 10. Or set a fixed cost that ignores the spell's level.
* Powers: switch any power off if you find it too strong. It becomes an ordinary spell again, needing spell stock, and keeps its level. Switching it back on makes it innate again and clears its spell stock. You can also change the Psicraft level that unlocks each power; this book lists them in that order.

* AutoCombat bonuses: on top of whichever strategy you choose, each power's bonus (Telekinesis +20 by default) raises how often auto-combat picks it. Can be switched off.

{p}

<b>Settings: Hurl and Strike</b>

* Psi-Hurl cost: a multiplier of the current or natural Telekinesis cost, or a fixed cost. Full flight distance can be switched on.
* AutoCombat throws loose objects or enemies into enemies, never through allies, neutrals or you; otherwise it hurls an enemy randomly, away or toward you. Enemies beside you are left to cheaper Telekinesis. Throwing allies is optional.
* Each power has an AutoCombat checkbox. Psi-Strike's cost is adjustable.
* Sound: the Telekinesis sound can be muted, or swapped per power for another sound.
* Lost this book? Psion settings give you a new copy, once a day.

Back up your save before changing powers.
