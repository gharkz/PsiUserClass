The Psion's Primer,an unnamed Psion

Few have heard of us, and fewer still can teach what we do. Our power is not drawn from magic. It comes from the mind itself.

<b>Psicraft</b>

Psicraft is our discipline. It grows as you use your powers, and as it grows, new powers awaken in you. No trainer can teach it.

Spellbooks of powers you already command will not grant you stock. Their insight flows into your Psicraft instead.

{p}

<b>Awakening</b>

* Psicraft 1: Telekinesis
* Psicraft 5: Psi-Arrow
* Psicraft 10: Psi-Bolt
* Psicraft 15: Kinetic Arrow
* Psicraft 20: Kinetic Bolt
* Psicraft 25: Psi-Shield
* Psicraft 35: Psi-Ball
* Psicraft 45: Psi-Funnel, Kinetic Ball
* Psicraft 50: Psi-Teleport
* Psicraft 55: Kinetic Funnel

Your powers are innate. They cost mana, never stock.

{p}

<b>Mind and force</b>

Psi powers strike the mind. They may confuse a foe or lull it to sleep, which leaves it open to your staff or blade.

Kinetic powers strike with raw force. Armor blunts them as it would any blow.

{p}

<b>The dead remember</b>

Never reach into the mind of the dead. Whatever harm your mind would deal them lashes back into you.

Telekinesis and kinetic powers are safe against them. In a Psion's hands, Telekinesis strikes twice as hard as any spellbook could teach. Wands, rods and scrolls carry their own power and are safe as well.

{p}

<b>Necropathy</b>

With practice, a Psion learns to touch dead minds without harm.

* Necropathy (Psicraft 10): the dead no longer lash back.
* Intermediate Necropathy (Psicraft 20): your mind reaches them at half strength.
* Master Necropathy (Psicraft 30): your mind reaches them fully.

Rare equipment is said to carry this gift as well.

{p}

<b>Psionic Edge</b>

Your mind steadies your hands. Harvesting tools bite harder, and the crafts and fine skills you have learned come easier. The edge grows slowly with Psicraft:

* Psicraft 1: +1
* Psicraft 5: +2
* Psicraft 10: +3
* Psicraft 20: +4
* Psicraft 30: +5
* Psicraft 52: +6
* Psicraft 100: +7
* Psicraft 200: +8

{p}

<b>Settings</b>

The strategy view on your character sheet has a Psion settings button.

* Auto-combat Telekinesis: don't use (default), pull distant foes only, hurl adjacent foes only, or both. Hurls never go through allies.
* Telekinesis collision dice sides: x2 by default; choose x1, x1.5, x2, x2.5 or x3.
* Powers: switch any power off if you find it too strong. It becomes an ordinary spell again, needing spell stock, and keeps its level. Switching it back on makes it innate again and clears its spell stock.

Back up your save before changing powers.
